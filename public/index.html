How it works

No coding needed. Four simple steps, with full source traceability and human control.

1

You describe

Type what should happen, like “a new user can sign up and see their dashboard”.

2

Agent studies the site

It visits pages and saves Source IDs, URLs, and UTC retrieval times as evidence.

3

You approve

Proposed code and decision checks are shown before execution. Nothing runs without your yes.

4

Proof, twice

It runs the test two times with fresh synthetic data to guarantee consistency without duplicates.
